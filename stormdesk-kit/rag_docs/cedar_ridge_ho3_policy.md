# Cedar Ridge Insurance Company - Homeowners HO-3 Policy Wording

This policy agreement sets forth the coverage, conditions, exclusions, and settlement terms for residential properties insured under the HO-3 Special Form by Cedar Ridge Insurance Company.

## Section I.1 Coverage A Dwelling
We cover the dwelling on the Described Location shown in the Declarations, including structures attached to the dwelling, used principally as a private residence. The limit of liability for Coverage A Dwelling is the specific limit shown on the declarations page. This limit represents the maximum amount payable for direct physical loss to the dwelling per covered occurrence, subject to applicable deductibles and policy endorsements.

## Section I.3 Perils Insured Against
We insure against direct physical loss to property described in Coverage A Dwelling and Coverage B Other Structures. Covered perils include direct physical loss caused by windstorm, hail, fire, lightning, explosion, riot, civil commotion, aircraft, vehicles, smoke, vandalism, and malicious mischief. Direct physical loss caused by windstorm and hail is covered subject to the specific deductibles, exclusions, and settlement provisions within this policy.

## Section I.4 Exclusions
We do not insure for loss caused directly or indirectly by any of the following excluded perils, regardless of any other cause or event contributing concurrently or in any sequence to the loss:
1. Flood and surface water, including waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, or spray, whether driven by wind or not.
2. Wear and tear, marring, or deterioration.
3. Neglect, meaning neglect of the insured to save and preserve property at and after the time of a loss.
4. Earth movement, including earthquake, landslide, mudslide, or earth sinking, rising, or shifting.

## Section I.5 Windstorm or Hail Deductible
A specific Windstorm or Hail Deductible applies to all direct physical losses caused by windstorm or hail. The Windstorm or Hail Deductible is calculated as 1% of the Coverage A limit per occurrence, as stated on the policy declarations page. This percentage deductible replaces the standard all-peril deductible for windstorm or hail losses. No claim for windstorm or hail damage is payable until covered loss exceeds this calculated deductible amount.

## Section I.6 Loss Settlement
Covered property losses are settled at replacement cost without deduction for depreciation, subject to the Coverage A policy limit, provided property is repaired or replaced with materials of like kind and quality, except as modified by endorsement CR-RA15.

## Endorsement CR-RA15 Roof Surfacing Actual Cash Value
Notwithstanding Section I.6 Loss Settlement, if the roof on the insured dwelling is more than 15 years old at the date of loss, loss or damage to roof surfacing materials will be settled at actual cash value rather than replacement cost. Actual cash value includes a deduction for physical depreciation based on roof age. Depreciation is calculated deterministically as:
Depreciation = Roof Portion Valuation x Minimum(0.80, Roof Age / 25)
Where Roof Age is measured in full years from installation to the date of loss.

## Section II.2 Duties After Loss
In the event of a loss to covered property, the insured must see that the following duties are performed:
1. Give prompt notice of the loss or damage to us or our agent.
2. Protect the property from further damage, make reasonable and necessary repairs, and keep an accurate record of repair expenses.
3. Cooperate with us in the investigation of the claim and provide requested records, receipts, and invoices.

## Section II.7 Concealment or Fraud
We do not provide coverage for any insured who has intentionally concealed or misrepresented any material fact or circumstance, engaged in fraudulent conduct, or made false statements relating to this insurance, whether before or after a loss.

## Section II.9 Policy Period
This policy applies only to direct physical loss or damage that occurs during the policy period shown on the declarations page.
