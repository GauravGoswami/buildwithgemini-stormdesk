# Cedar Ridge Insurance Company - Automobile Comprehensive Policy Wording

This policy contract outlines the formal terms, coverages, limits, conditions, and deductible provisions applicable to Comprehensive Coverage under personal automobile insurance policies issued by Cedar Ridge Insurance Company.

## Section C.1 Comprehensive Coverage
We will pay for direct and accidental physical loss to your covered automobile, including its factory-installed equipment, caused by a cause of loss other than collision. For the purposes of this insurance policy contract, comprehensive loss includes, but is not limited to, direct physical damage resulting from missiles or falling objects, fire, theft or larceny, explosion, earthquake, windstorm, hail, water, flood, malicious mischief or vandalism, riot or civil commotion, contact with bird or animal, or breakage of glass. Direct physical losses caused directly by severe weather events, including straight-line windstorms, microbursts, fallen tree branches or limbs, and hail impacts during convective storm events, are fully covered under Comprehensive Coverage subject to all applicable policy terms and conditions.

## Section C.3 Limit of Liability
Our total limit of liability for direct physical loss or damage under Comprehensive Coverage shall not exceed the lesser of the following two amounts:
1. The actual cash value (ACV) of the stolen or damaged covered automobile at the time of the loss, taking into account appropriate adjustments for physical depreciation, prior wear and tear, age, mileage, and pre-existing cosmetic or mechanical condition.
2. The total amount necessary to repair or replace the damaged vehicle or its parts with other property of like kind and quality, utilizing original equipment manufacturer (OEM) or qualified aftermarket parts where permitted under applicable state regulations and guidelines.

In the event of a total loss determination, where the estimated cost of repair plus salvage recovery exceeds the established actual cash value, claim settlement will be executed on an actual cash value basis less the applicable deductible.

## Section C.4 Deductible
Our specific financial obligation to pay for comprehensive loss or damage to a covered automobile applies only to the portion of covered loss that exceeds the comprehensive deductible shown on the policy declarations page. The flat comprehensive deductible (such as $500 or $1,000) applies separately and distinctly to each individual loss event or occurrence. The insured policyholder remains fully responsible for satisfying the full deductible amount before Cedar Ridge Insurance Company disburses any claim settlement funds or authorizes vehicle repair payments.
