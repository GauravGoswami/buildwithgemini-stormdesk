# Cedar Ridge Insurance Company - Catastrophe Event Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

This Standard Operating Procedure governs claims handling, workflow prioritization, resource allocation, and emergency operational protocols during declared catastrophe (CAT) events managed by Cedar Ridge Insurance Company.

## Section 1 Declaration
Cedar Ridge Insurance Company executive management has formally issued an official catastrophe declaration designated as CAT-2024-05-HOU in direct response to the severe Houston Derecho event occurring on 16 May 2024. This catastrophe declaration encompasses Harris County, Texas, along with immediately surrounding contiguous counties impacted by severe straight-line windstorms, tornadic gusts, microbursts, and intense convective weather systems. All insurance claims associated with loss dates between 15 May 2024 and 21 May 2024 within the designated impacted geographic zone shall be officially tagged under catastrophe code `houston-derecho-2024-05-16` for tracking, reporting, resource allocation, and surge processing purposes across all operating claims handling departments and emergency response units.

## Section 2 Surge Triage Order
During a declared catastrophe surge event, claims adjusters, supervisory personnel, field operations teams, and automated triage decision workflows must process incoming claims strictly according to the following mandatory priority queue order:
1. **Uninhabitable Homes First**: Immediate operational priority is assigned to severe structural property losses where residential dwellings are rendered unsafe, structurally compromised, or uninhabitable, requiring urgent temporary emergency living expense advances, temporary shelter arrangements, emergency debris removal, and emergency roof tarping services to prevent secondary water damage.
2. **SIU Screening**: Secondary priority is given to screening incoming claims for potential fraud indicators, coverage discrepancies, opportunistic claims, and SIU red flags before claim settlement funds are committed or disbursed by claim handlers.
3. **Fast-Track Processing**: Tertiary priority is assigned to standard fast-track claims eligible for rapid automated settlement disbursal, minor property damage evaluations, and expedited processing to maintain overall workflow velocity, operational efficiency, and high customer satisfaction during severe catastrophe surges.

## Section 3 Fast-Track Target
For all property and casualty claims designated under the `fast_track` triage decision during a declared catastrophe surge event, Cedar Ridge Insurance Company commits to issuing official claim payment disbursal within 72 hours of the formal triage decision confirmation by the handling adjuster.

## Section 4 Post-Declaration Policies
Any insurance policy incepting after the official catastrophe declaration date that subsequently reports a claim loss occurring within 7 days of policy inception requires mandatory senior adjuster review and physical field inspection prior to any claim approval or payment disbursal.
