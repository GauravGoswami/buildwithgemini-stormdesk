# Texas Prompt Payment of Claims Statutory Summary - Texas Insurance Code Chapter 542

This document summarizes statutory requirements and compliance deadlines under Texas Insurance Code Chapter 542, Subchapter B (Payment of Claims), governing insurer obligations in the State of Texas.

## Section 542.055 Notice of Claim
Not later than the 15th day after the date an insurer receives notice of a claim, the insurer shall:
1. Acknowledge receipt of the claim in writing.
2. Commence any investigation of the claim.
3. Request from the claimant all items, statements, and forms that the insurer reasonably believes, at that time, will be required from the claimant.

## Section 542.056 Acceptance or Rejection of Claims
Except as provided by Section 542.059, an insurer shall notify a claimant in writing of the acceptance or rejection of a claim not later than the 15th business day after the date the insurer receives all items, statements, and forms required by the insurer to secure final proof of loss. If the insurer has a reasonable basis to believe that the loss resulted from arson, the insurer shall notify the claimant of acceptance or rejection not later than the 30th day after receiving required proof of loss. If the insurer is unable to accept or reject the claim within the specified period, the insurer may extend the deadline up to 45 calendar days by providing written notice to the claimant explaining the specific reasons additional time is needed.

## Section 542.057 Payment of Claim
If an insurer notifies a claimant under Section 542.056 that the insurer will pay a claim or part of a claim, the insurer shall pay the claim not later than the 5th business day after the date notice is given.

## Section 542.059 Extension of Deadlines
In the event of a weather-related catastrophe or natural disaster as defined or declared by the Texas Commissioner of Insurance, the statutory claim handling deadlines specified under Chapter 542 are extended for an additional 15 calendar days.

## Section 542.060 Liability for Violation
If an insurer delays payment of a claim following receipt of all required proof of loss items for more than the statutory period, the insurer is liable to pay the holder of the policy simple interest on the amount of the claim at the rate of 18 percent per annum, together with reasonable and necessary attorney fees incurred in enforcing the statutory obligation.
